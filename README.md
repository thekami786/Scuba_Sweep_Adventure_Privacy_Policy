# Privacy Policy for Scuba Sweep Adventure

**Last Updated:** June, 2026

Welcome to **Scuba Sweep Adventure** (referred to as "the Game"), developed by **The Kami**. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a safe gaming environment. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in connection with the Game.

By installing and playing the Game, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use

### Personal Data
We do not directly collect, request, or store any personal identifier information (such as your real name, address, phone number, or government IDs). 

### Gameplay Data
The Game uses local storage on your device (via local settings and preferences) to cache your chosen in-game nickname and track your high scores.

## 2. Third-Party Services and Data Processing

To optimize gameplay performance, support in-game features, and support monetization, the Game integrates third-party services that may collect and process device-specific information (such as IP addresses, advertising identifiers, and device details) subject to their respective privacy policies:

*   **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob):** Used to display advertisements. AdMob may use device advertising identifiers to serve personalized and non-personalized ads, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance.
    *   [Google Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
*   **Google Firebase Services (Firebase Analytics, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Auth):** Used for analytics, real-time leaderboard syncing, and secure user session caching.
    *   [Firebase Privacy and Security Policies](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
*   **Google Play Services:** Used to support user sign-in, cloud saves, achievements, and leaderboard services.
    *   [Google Play Services Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## 3. Children's Privacy

The Game is designed to be family-friendly and fully complies with the **Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)** and regional child protection guidelines:
*   We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
*   If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information to third-party services integrated with our game, please contact us immediately, and we will take appropriate actions to remove such data.
